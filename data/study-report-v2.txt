================================================================
WHAT THIS DATA CANNOT SUPPORT
================================================================
The previous post also claimed:
  - "3.4x citation lift for sites with a valid llms.txt"
  - "FAQ schema 6.1x more likely to be cited for question queries"
  - "HowTo schema 3.8x more likely to be cited for step-by-step queries"
  - "the top 3 fixes deliver ~80% of the score improvement"

All four are claims about whether an AI engine CITED a site. A static
crawler audit cannot observe citation. Measuring them needs either a
multi-engine citation crawl or the LLM simulation in worker/visibility.js,
which is an estimate rather than a measurement. They are therefore NOT
reproducible from this dataset and are not restated here.
