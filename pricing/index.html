Pricing FAQ

Is GeoScore really free?

Yes. Single URL audits, the CLI, and the MCP Server are free forever with no signup. The source code is MIT-licensed on GitHub.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No credit card required for the Free plan. The Pro plan includes a 7-day free trial — your card is only charged after the trial ends.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from the customer portal. You keep access until the end of your billing period.

How is GeoScore different from GeoReady?

GeoScore is open-source (MIT), supports Chinese, includes CLI and MCP Server, and offers prompt injection detection — all for free. GeoReady charges $9-89/month for similar monitoring features.

Do you handle VAT and taxes?

Yes. As a Merchant of Record, our payment partner Creem handles VAT, GST, and sales tax compliance in 190+ countries. You don't need to worry about tax forms.

When will Studio and Agency be available?

Studio and Agency are coming soon. We're launching with Pro first to validate the workflow. Watch our GitHub repository for updates.
