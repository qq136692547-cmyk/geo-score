Pricing FAQ

Is GeoScore really free?

Single browser audits, the CLI, and the MCP Server are free. Sign-in is optional; Pro monitoring and history are paid. The source code is MIT-licensed on GitHub.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No credit card required for the Free plan. Pro monthly includes a 7-day free trial — your card is only charged after the trial ends. Pro annual is paid upfront for $79 and does not include a trial.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from the customer portal. You keep access until the end of your billing period.

How is GeoScore different from closed-source GEO monitoring tools?

GeoScore is open-source (MIT): a CLI and MCP Server you can point at any LLM endpoint, plus prompt injection detection in the free browser audit. Closed-source monitoring suites are billed as monthly per-domain subscriptions and keep their scoring logic closed. They report what AI engines say about you; GeoScore audits what your site gives those engines to work with — 11 dimensions, with fix files you can apply yourself.

Do you handle VAT and taxes?

Yes. As a Merchant of Record, our payment partner Creem handles VAT, GST, and sales tax compliance in 190+ countries. You don't need to worry about tax forms.

Can you just do the fixes for me?

Yes. The AI Visibility Fix Pack is a one-time $49 done-for-you service: a custom llms.txt built from your catalog, explicit AI-crawler rules for robots.txt, Product schema for one best-seller, a 1-page audit, and a re-audit after you deploy. Communicate directly via email (hello@geoscore.help) with a free diagnosis first, delivered in 48 hours.

Are Studio and Agency plans available?

Yes. Studio ($29/mo) and Agency ($59/mo) plans are available with 7-day free trials. They include higher audit frequencies, more domains, and team features.
